# Business Modules

## Accounts
Users, sessions, roles, permissions, lockout, password lifecycle and audit events.

## Customers
Customer master records, contact information, search, status and customer history.

## Inventory
Products, SKUs, prices, quantities, stock movements and inventory reporting.

## Sales
Orders, order lines, pricing, taxes, totals, payments, fulfillment state and cancellation.

## Accounting
Chart of accounts, ledger entries, debits, credits, balances, receivables and payables.

## Invoices
Invoice headers, lines, taxes, totals, payment state, due dates and voiding.

## Tickets
Support tickets, assignment, messages, status, priority and closure.

## Email
Outbound and inbound message metadata, recipients, queues and attachment metadata.

## Reports
Parameterized report requests and text/tabular output. Future output adapters may include CSV and spreadsheet formats.

## Analytics
Aggregations used for business dashboards: totals, averages, trends, distributions and financial metrics.

## Calculator
Reusable business calculations for tax, profit, margin, ROI, break-even, loans and cash flow.
